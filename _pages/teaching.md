## BAE 401/501: Sensors and Controls

**Term:** Spring Semesters (2027 - )
**Level:** Undergraduate / Graduate
**Role:** Instructor

### Course Description

Basic concepts of sensors and controls for biological systems. Study of transducers and circuits utilized in biological and agricultural engineering applications. Demonstration of concepts of error, accuracy and precision, linearity and other instrument characteristics by electronic models. Provision of hands-on experience for reinforcing lecture concepts in laboratories.

**Prerequisites:** BAE 305 (or instructor consent)
**Credit:** Will not be given for both BAE 401 and BAE 501
